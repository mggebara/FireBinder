## Rubric (1-5)

Significant events are things like evacuations ordered or lifted, structures lost, injuries or
fatalities, major runs or growth, line holding or containment milestones, road closures, red flag
warnings and other weather that drives fire behavior, resource changes, cause.

- 5: covers every significant event in the published narrative; invents nothing.
- 4: misses one minor event, or adds one minor detail not in the reference; nothing important wrong.
- 3: misses one significant event, or covers the main picture with several minor gaps.
- 2: misses several significant events, or invents or misstates a significant event.
- 1: describes a materially different situation from the published 209, or is empty.

If the published narrative is missing or empty, judge only whether the draft is internally
coherent and consistent with the field table, and say so in the rationale.
