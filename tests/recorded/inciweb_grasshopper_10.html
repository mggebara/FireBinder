Seeding Project on the Grasshopper Fire
Sun, 09/27/2026 - 09:54
Seeding Project on the Grasshopper Fire

This burnt and heavily disturbed area of forest was used to park vehicles, stage equipment, and store slash piles during the Grasshopper Fire. Crews spread a native seed mixture in the soil and placed straw and timber slash on top, using 120 pounds of seed and a half ton of straw and slash. The cover material helps maintain higher moisture levels, encourages germination, decreases likelihood that the seed will be eaten or blown away, and minimize erosion. These native plants also germinate quickly, reducing the likelihood that invasive plants will establish within the area.

Grasshopper Information Map 10.1.26
Thu, 10/01/2026 - 09:50
Grasshopper Information Map 10.1.26

The Grasshopper Fire was first reported 7/23/26, following multiple lightning storms over the forest in the preceding two weeks. It started north of Forest Road 4812 and west of Rocky Butte. It quickly spread east and north following winds and hot, dry conditions.

Current evacuation levels are at: https://perimetermap.com/wascocounty-or

There is a Closure Order in effect on Mt. Hood National Forest: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mthood/alerts/closure-place-grasshopper-fire

Fuels Involved

Brush (2 feet) 
Hardwood Litter 
Closed Timber Litter

Three fuel transitions exist across the fire footprint: high-elevation Cascade mixed conifer on the western portion; mid-elevation Ponderosa pine with light understory in the center; and lower-elevation Central Oregon grass/brush on the eastern flank. BI and ERC are below seasonal average values.
Significant Events

Minimal, smoldering, creeping

Observed fire activity was minimal with smoldering mainly on the southwest portion of the fire in areas of heavy fuels. Humidity recovery in the western areas has been good. Burn periods have been limited to a few hours in the midafternoon.
Outlook
Planned Actions

Suppression repair activities will continue across the incident. All divisions: Chipping, log skidding/decking/hauling, isolated falling of hazard trees, and repair of control lines.
Remarks

Mt Hood Forest Closure Order, No. 06-06-01-26-18, became effective on October 1, 2026.

TFR was terminated at 1800 hrs on 09/28/2026.

Level 1 evacuation notices were lifted in Wasco County on 09/29/2026.
